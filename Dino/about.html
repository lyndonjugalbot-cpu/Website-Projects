Our story

More than a marketplace

DinoMart is a family, a movement, and a mission-driven force for good — built by collectors, for collectors.

Built by the DinoFam

DinoMart was established by the team behind the DinoFam YouTube channel — a team of dedicated veterans, creators, entrepreneurs and dreamers. We built this store on three values: integrity, service and innovation.

Every product is hand-selected for authenticity and quality. If it carries a licence, it's genuine. If it says "Chase", that's what ships. And if something isn't right, our knowledgeable staff will make it right — fast.

Around the store grew DinoFam: a tight-knit community bound by a shared commitment to support, uplift and empower one another. #VibeJurassic isn't a slogan — it's how we show up for each other.
