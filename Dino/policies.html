Shipping Policy

In-stock orders are dispatched from our warehouse within 1–2 business days. A tracking number is emailed the moment your shipping label is generated.

Delivery estimates

  • United States: 3–7 business days (standard), 2–3 business days (expedited).
  • Canada: 5–10 business days.
  • International (27+ countries): 7–18 business days. Duties and import taxes are calculated at checkout where available; otherwise they are the responsibility of the recipient.

Shipping cost

Flat-rate shipping is $6.95 within the US. Orders over $75 ship free. Rates for other destinations are shown at checkout in your local currency.

Lost or damaged parcels

If your order arrives damaged, email a photo to sales@dinofam.io within 7 days and we'll ship a replacement or issue a refund — no return required for damaged goods.

Refund & Return Policy

We want you to love your haul. Unopened items in original packaging may be returned within 30 days of delivery for a full refund to the original payment method.

  • Start a return by emailing sales@dinofam.io with your order number.
  • Return shipping is paid by the customer unless the item was defective, damaged, or sent in error.
  • Refunds are processed within 3–5 business days of us receiving the return; your bank may take a few days more to post it.
  • Sealed trading-card products, opened blind boxes, final-sale and numbered limited-edition items are non-returnable unless faulty.

Funko Chase variants: when a listing says "Chase Variant", the 1-in-6 Chase is what ships. Common listings ship the common edition.

Privacy Policy

DinoMart collects only what we need to fulfil your order and improve your experience: your name, contact details, shipping/billing address, and order history. Payment card data is processed by our PCI-compliant payment provider and is never stored on our servers.

How we use your data

  • To process, ship and support your orders.
  • To send order and shipping notifications.
  • To send marketing emails (drops, restocks, offers) — only if you opt in, and you can unsubscribe from any email.
  • To detect and prevent fraud.

Your choices

You can request a copy of your data, ask us to correct it, or ask us to delete it, by emailing sales@dinofam.io. This demo store keeps your cart, wishlist and preferences in your browser's local storage only — clearing your browser data removes them.
