How a Commission Works

From reference to shelf

A clear, five-step path. You approve the piece at every gate before it moves forward.

  1. 01

    Brief & Quote

    You send references, scale, pose and base ideas. We reply with a fixed quote, timeline and a deposit link.

  2. 02

    Sculpt & Approve

    The figure is digitally sculpted or kitbashed. You review turntables and request changes before printing.

  3. 03

    Resin Print & Cure

    Printed in high-detail resin, washed, UV-cured, then cleaned up and pinned for a solid assembly.

  4. 04

    Prime & Paint

    Hand priming, airbrush base coats, oil blending, detail brushwork, and weathering to match the reference.

  5. 05

    Seal & Ship

    Matte and gloss varnish passes, base mounting, then padded crating and tracked shipping to your door.
