The shop

No rushed cuts.
No guesswork.

Smart Buzz is a neighbourhood barbershop built around one idea: you should leave looking exactly how you pictured it. Every seat starts with a proper consult, then clippers, scissors and a straight razor do the rest.

From tight skin fades to relaxed scissor cuts, beard sculpting and first haircuts for the little ones — the standard doesn't change. Clean tools, honest advice and a finish that holds its shape for weeks.

  • Consultation before every cut
  • Fresh blades & sanitised tools, every client
  • Walk-ins welcome, bookings recommended
  • Kid-friendly chair and cape
  • Everyone in the chair — LGBTQ+ friendly
