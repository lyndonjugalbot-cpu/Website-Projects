04About PCMOTOH

Started as a hobby.
Built into a business.

PCMOTOH began the way most great builds do — at a desk at home, chasing lower temps and higher frame rates. Years of building, tuning and fixing machines turned that passion into PCMOTOH Computer Trading: a shop in Consolacion where every PC still gets a hobbyist’s attention to detail.

Whether it’s your first gaming rig, a workstation for school or a fleet of BPO seats, you’ll get the same thing: straight advice, fair prices and work we’re proud to put our name on.

  • Customer service

    Plain-language answers, no jargon, and help that doesn’t end at the counter.

  • Honest pricing

    Itemised quotes at fair, current prices. No upsells you don’t need.

  • Reliable service

    Quality parts, careful work and machines tested before they go home.
