The Process

No AI.
Just skill.

Every character starts as a pencil sketch. WOTS Creative Studio is built on traditional illustration fundamentals — hand-drawn linework, digital inking, and color rendered layer by layer. If a brief could be solved by typing a prompt, this isn't that studio. This is for brands and stories that deserve a real artist's hand.

1
Sketch

Rough pencils to lock composition, pose, and story before anything else.

2
Ink

Clean linework built by hand — every edge intentional, every character consistent.

3
Color & Light

Full rendering pass: color, shading, and atmosphere to bring the piece to life.

4
Deliver

Print-ready files for packaging, or high-res art for prints, merch, and brand use.
